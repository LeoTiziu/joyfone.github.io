    Two ways to connect

    The same app works with your computer and your TV. Pick one on the start screen.

    PC · Windows 10 and 11

    Play on your computer

    The Windows receiver makes the PC see your phone as an Xbox 360 or DualShock 4 controller, so it works with games that support those controllers.

    • Direct Bluetooth link, no Wi-Fi
    • The PC approves each phone the first time
    • Up to 4 phones on one PC

    Bluetooth RFCOMM · receiver + ViGEmBus driver

    TV · new

    Control and play on your TV

    Your phone shows up on the TV as a Bluetooth keyboard, media keys and game controller. Pair it once from the TV settings and you are done.

    • Nothing to install on the TV
    • Remote with arrows, OK, volume and media keys
    • The same on-screen gamepad, now on the TV

    Bluetooth HID · Android TV and Google TV

    A real controller on your screen

    Two sticks, a D-pad, analog triggers and every button, with multi-touch. Each mode has its own layout.

    Standard

    The full controller, for any genre.

    2 sticks · D-pad · analog LT/RT

    Racing

    Tilt the phone like a steering wheel to turn. Big triggers to accelerate and brake.

    RT gas · LT brake · tilt steering

    Gun

    Aim by moving the phone. A quick jerk reloads, and the phone vibrates on every shot.

    gyro aiming · RT fire · LT aim

    TV mode

    The remote that never gets lost in the couch

    The remote screen holds the keys you use every day. Hold an arrow to scroll a list, or hold volume to keep it going. When you want to play, tap “Play on TV” and the phone becomes a game controller.

    • Videos
    • Movies
    • Shows
    • Music
    • Games
    • News

    Try the remote: arrows pick, OK opens.

    TV mode works with devices that accept Bluetooth keyboards and controllers, such as TVs running Android TV or Google TV. Not every smart TV accepts a Bluetooth game controller. This feature is in beta; the list of tested TVs is in the FAQ.

    Beta

    Pick up your phone and play

    Joyfone is in beta. Get the app on your Android phone and, if you play on a PC, the Windows receiver. Older versions and notes: releases page.

    Download Android app (APK, 19 MB) Download Windows receiver (67 MB)

    To install the APK, Android asks you to allow installs from this source. The Windows receiver comes as a .zip: extract it and open Joyfone.exe. It also needs the free ViGEmBus driver; see How to connect.
