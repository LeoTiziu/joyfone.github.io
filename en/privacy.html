Privacy policy

In short: Joyfone has no account, no ads, collects no usage data and sends nothing to any server. The Android app does not even request internet permission.

Android app

  • Connection: the app talks only over Bluetooth to the PC or TV you chose. Your touches and phone movements go straight to that device and are not recorded.
  • Permissions: “Nearby devices” (Android 12+) to use paired devices; in TV mode, permission to make the phone visible for 2 minutes when you ask; vibration. The app does not use location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • Data on the phone: settings, layouts, the Bluetooth address of the last device used and the background photo you pick stay in the app's own storage. None of it is backed up to the cloud. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.
  • Sensors: the gyroscope and tilt sensor are on only on the game screen, when a motion feature is enabled. Readings become stick movement and are not stored.

Windows receiver

  • Works only over Bluetooth, with no internet and no account.
  • Stores on the PC the list of approved phones (name, Bluetooth identifier and approval date), your chosen appearance and technical connection logs for troubleshooting. You can delete its data folder at any time.

This website

  • The site uses no cookies and no analytics.
  • The site's fonts load from Google Fonts. When you open a page, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address, as with any website visit.
  • The controller tester reads your controller only in your browser and sends nothing.
  • In the donation section, your browser fetches the dollar exchange rate from a public service (AwesomeAPI or ExchangeRate-API), which receives your IP address as with any request. The QR code is generated in your browser. The donation itself happens on Nubank's Pix payment page, under Nubank's privacy policy; this site receives no payment data.

Contact

Privacy questions: open a message at github.com/LeoTiziu/joyfone.github.io/issues.

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
