How to connect

Install the app on your Android phone (version 9 or newer) and connect it to a Windows PC. After installation, none of this uses the internet.

PC · Windows 10 (2004+) and 11

On the PC

The PC needs Bluetooth, the Joyfone Receiver and the free ViGEmBus driver.

  1. Install the ViGEmBus driver from the project's official releases page on GitHub (nefarius/ViGEmBus). Joyfone never installs drivers by itself and never asks you to turn off antivirus or Secure Boot.

  2. Extract the receiver and open Joyfone.exe. It includes everything it needs to run and does not need administrator rights. Check that it shows “Joyfone service: advertised”.

  3. Pair the phone with the PC once: in Windows, Settings › Bluetooth & devices › Add device › Bluetooth, then confirm the code on both devices.

  4. On the phone, open Joyfone, pick the PC from the paired list and tap Connect.

  5. On the PC, click Allow this phone. You are only asked the first time.

  6. On the phone, choose Xbox or PlayStation 4, the game mode, and tap Play.

Some games only detect the new controller after a restart. Games with anti-cheat may block virtual controllers. To check buttons and sticks, use the controller tester.
